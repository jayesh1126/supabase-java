# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Be open to feedback
- Focus on what is best for the community

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or inflammatory comments

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this code of conduct and may remove or reject contributions that do not align with these guidelines.

---

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
